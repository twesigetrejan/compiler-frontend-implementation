Operator-Precedence Table (Shift vs. Reduce decisions)

When a binary operator is on the stack and another is the lookahead, the parser compares their precedence levels to decide whether to Shift (push the lookahead) or Reduce (fold the stack).

Stack op \ Lookahead+-*/)$
+ (prec=1)RRSSRR
- (prec=1)RRSSRR
* (prec=2)RRRRRR
/ (prec=2)RRRRRR

S = Shift  (lookahead binds tighter)  |  R = Reduce  (stack operator has equal or higher precedence)
